# FFD 2.0 RC1.0.14.8.9.9.4.1.3.12.10.5.1 — Media Filename Suggestions

Adds read-only filename suggestions to Improve → Media → Non-standard filename. Existing Reunion person/event/family media associations are primary evidence. One clear person association produces a High-confidence `Surname, First names` suggestion; multiple-person associations remain Review. Descriptive filename text is preserved conservatively. No files are renamed and no Reunion references are changed.
