# FFD 2.0 RC1.0.14.8.9.9.4.1.3.12.8 — Structure-Driven Family History Publishing

Family History publishing is now driven by the selected genealogy structure.

- Removes the paternal endpoint from the Family History workflow.
- Removes the manual branch-order changeover control.
- Existing saved family and terminal-individual selections remain usable; legacy endpoint/order settings are ignored.
- Selected family chapters publish in depth-first genealogical family order, with siblings ordered by recorded birth date.
- Each branch ends naturally where selection ends.
