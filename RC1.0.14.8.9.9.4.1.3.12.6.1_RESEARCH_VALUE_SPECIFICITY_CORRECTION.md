# FFD 2.0 RC1.0.14.8.9.9.4.1.3.12.6.1 — Research Value Specificity Correction

- Tightens Research Value so broad/common-name Ryerson result sets are not automatically promoted as High opportunity.
- High missing-Death opportunities now require a recent (within 100 years) and specific candidate set (one or two current candidates).
- Three-to-five candidates are reduced to Potential opportunity; larger recent sets require careful review.
- Old (>100 year) missing-Death leads are reduced even when otherwise plausible.
- Exact normalized Death-date agreement with an existing unsourced Reunion Death remains the strongest High opportunity signal.
- Current candidate count, confidence and recency now contribute to ordering inside the explainable value bands.
