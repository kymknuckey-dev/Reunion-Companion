# FFD 2.0 RC1.0.14.8.9.9.4.1.3.12.6.5 — Review-State Match Grading Correction

Discovery Match Grading now evaluates the candidates supplied by the active review-state view rather than hard-coding `state=new`. This keeps match quality visible after a candidate is accepted, deferred, rejected, waiting for Reunion, or confirmed complete. New discovery grading and grouped review behaviour are unchanged.
