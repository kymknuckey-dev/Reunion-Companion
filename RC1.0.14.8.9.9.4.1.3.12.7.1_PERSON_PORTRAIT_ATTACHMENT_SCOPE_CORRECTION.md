# FFD 2.0 RC1.0.14.8.9.9.4.1.3.12.7.1 — Person Portrait Attachment Scope Correction

- Person header portraits now come only from media directly attached to the person.
- Event media such as burial/headstone photographs can no longer be promoted to a person portrait.
- History Book portrait selection follows the same attachment-scope rule.
- Preferred direct person photographs still win; otherwise the first ordinary direct person photograph remains the fallback.
