# FFD 2.0 RC1.0.14.8.9.9.4.1.3.12.6.2 — Discovery Match Grading

This release pivots External Evidence Review from a synthetic Research Value ordering to visible identity-match grading.

- Strong match: Ryerson birth date agrees with Reunion.
- Supported match: structured place and/or family detail corroborates identity.
- Possible match: name-based candidate with limited corroboration.
- Low specificity: broad name-only candidate set.
- The newspaper/publication is displayed as context but is not treated by itself as identity proof.
- One strong candidate remains visible as strong even among many name-based candidates.
- Most Recent and Relationship remain the only sort modes.
