# FFD 1.9 Build 1 — Family Book Narrative & Layout Refinement

Principle: **Research = preserve; Presentation = present; Publication = narrate.**

- Couple chapter titles render husband / “and” / wife on separate centred lines.
- Redundant family summary boxes and duplicate marriage-place display are removed.
- About the Family uses reader-facing prose and natural child-count wording.
- Education & Working Life facts begin on separate lines.
- Reunion note line endings and paragraph structure are preserved for Research/Presentation paths.
- Publication can use the local Ollama model for restrained, evidence-grounded narrative; original notes remain unchanged and are the fallback if the model is unavailable.
- Descendant charts include spouses as members of descendant family units.
