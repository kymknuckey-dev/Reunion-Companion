# FFD 2.0 RC1.0.11.1 — Family Scope & Book Presentation QA Pass 1

RC1.0.11.1 refines the family-scope publishing experience after real-book QA.

## Changes

- Restores visible spinner/progress feedback while scoped HTML/PDF content is being created.
- Keeps the paternal family path as the default Book Section sequence.
- Shows children of paternal siblings in family/descendant chart context, then stops those collateral branches before another generation expands.
- Adds HTML links to copied original PDFs and images. Multi-page PDFs retain the natural single-page HTML preview while the original full PDF is directly available from the report.
- Renames **Spouse Family Context** to **Spouse Family & Descendants**.
- Removes boxed spouse-chart presentation and aligns it with the established **Family & Descendants** visual language.
- Groups the two family charts together, with **Family & Descendants** first and **Spouse Family & Descendants** immediately after it.
- Does not alter RC1.0.10 multi-page PDF print reproduction geometry.
