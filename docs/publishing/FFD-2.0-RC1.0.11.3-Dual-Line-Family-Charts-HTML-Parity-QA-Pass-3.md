# FFD 2.0 RC1.0.11.3 — Dual-Line Family Charts & HTML Parity QA Pass 3

## Scope

- Treat Family & Descendants as the master embedded-book chart template.
- Show both **Husband's Descendants** and **Wife's Descendants** above the immediate parent context.
- Use the same dual-line presentation in **Spouse Family & Descendants**.
- Visually distinguish the two sides while retaining one chart language and natural family grouping.
- Preserve the existing bounded book-chart traversal rules; do not expand the embedded charts indefinitely.
- Ensure HTML History Books contain the same Family & Descendants, Spouse Family & Descendants, Husband's Descendants and Wife's Descendants content as the PDF source HTML.
- Preserve all RC1.0.10 multi-page PDF reproduction behaviour.

## Future enhancement — standalone Family & Descendants report

Record, but do not implement in RC1.0.11.3, a standalone Family & Descendants report that can be generated independently of a History Book. The standalone report may later support deeper or all-level traversal, more genealogical detail, and presentation optimised for readers who specifically want the extended family structure.

The History Book version remains deliberately bounded.
