# FFD 2.0 RC1.0.13.1.1 — Reports Person Header Parity

Final navigation/person-context parity correction for the RC1.0.13 phase.

When Reports is opened from a selected person:
- show the normal person header in both Research and Presentation modes;
- keep Reports active under Output;
- retain the selected person's sidebar context;
- remove the redundant `Back to <person>` button.

When Reports is opened globally with no selected person:
- show no person header;
- show no Back-to-person control;
- retain Reports as the global Output destination.

No report-generation, publishing, genealogy, relationship, context-search or conversation behaviour changes.
